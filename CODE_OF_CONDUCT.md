# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pangostack to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful, also when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only for yourself.

## What we do not accept

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information of others, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at sebastian@squidex.io. Every report is reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code. Depending on the severity, the consequence ranges from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
